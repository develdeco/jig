## Verbs

### `jig recall [query...] [--json] [--store] [--project]`

Registered from `cmd/jig/recall.go`, Order 380. Positionals are the query,
joined with spaces; a bare `--` makes everything after it query text. Exit 0;
a store it cannot read is the usual refusal. It reads the store as it is on
this machine (no `Sync`) and writes nothing.

Blocks, a section left out when it has nothing (`tickets: none` is not
printed either; `duplicates: none` is, once a query was given):

- `recall:` (`query`, `open_tickets`, `shipped_tickets`)
- `tickets[N]{id,state,title}`: the open tickets (not published), in store
  order; `state` is the readiness state of `jig next` (new, ready, active,
  green, blocked)
- `edges[N]{ticket,blocked_by,kind,blocker}`: the blocked_by edges out of open
  tickets; `blocker` is `waiting`, `shipped` or `dangling`
- `duplicates[N]{ticket,score,in,state,title}`: open tickets whose title or
  body has token Jaccard of at least 0.5 with the query, best first
- `fog[N]{chart,item}`: the items of each `charts/*/map.md` fog section
- `platform[N]{path,title,bytes}` and `platform_lines[N]{path,kind,line}`
  (`head` lines: the first five; `match` lines: later lines that share a word
  with the query)
- `answers[N]{ticket,title,id,text}`: ledger.md answers
- one `help` block

`--json` prints one object: `query`, `tickets[{id,title,state}]`, `shipped`,
`edges[{ticket,blocked_by,kind,blocker_shipped,dangling}]`,
`duplicates[{ticket,title,state,score,in}]`, `fog[{chart,items,more}]`,
`platform[{path,title,bytes,head,matches}]`,
`answers[{ticket,title,answers[{id,text}]}]`. Every list is present, `[]`
when empty. `internal/recall.Report` is the type.

### `jig validate <ticket>` (changed)

- **Refused** (VALIDATION_ERROR, as before): everything it refused, plus an
  upstream cut edge: following ticket.yaml `blocked_by` from the ticket
  through the tickets it waits on (aliases resolved, published tickets not
  followed, cycles safe), an edge that names a ticket the store lacks is a
  problem "upstream ticket X, which T waits on: blocked_by "Y" is not a
  ticket in the store". The ticket own edges were already refused
  (`validateTicketDeps`); they are not reported twice.
- **Advised** (printed under a valid verdict, never a failure), table
  `advisories[N]{code,message,help}`: `SLICES_UNCONNECTED` (the slices split
  into groups no blocked_by edge connects, e.g. `a+b | c | d`) and
  `WORK_COVERED` (a slice goal has token Jaccard of at least 0.5 with the
  title of a shipped ticket, or of a ledger.md entry heading; at most three
  per slice; the ticket itself is left out).
- **Approval**, only when valid: `valid: yes`, then `approval:` (`result`,
  `by`, `email`, `at`, `provenance`, `brief_sha256`), then the `sections`
  table, the advisories, one help line. `result` is `recorded` (approval.yaml
  written, an `approval` journal line, a push) or `unchanged` (the brief is
  already approved as it is by a word at least as strong; nothing written).
  `by` and `email` come from `gitx.ResolveIdentity(store root)`, resolved
  before anything is written: a machine with no identity exits 1 with
  `IDENTITY_REQUIRED` and the store untouched. `provenance` is `person` when
  stdin is a terminal, else `agent` (`defaultProvenance`, as `note` and
  `answer` do). A person validating a brief an agent approved replaces the
  record (person outranks agent); an agent never replaces a person approval of
  the same brief. A changed brief is always re-approved.
- The hash is taken **before** the checks read brief.md, so a brief edited
  while validate runs is approved as the older text and reads as changed.
- The help line now says `jig next <T>` then `jig claim <T> --slice <slice>`
  (it said `jig run`); D13 need not touch it.

## Line shapes

`tickets/<id>/approval.yaml` (S0b `store.Approval`): `by`, `email`, `at`
(RFC 3339, second precision, `env.now`), `brief_sha256` (hex sha256 of
brief.md exactly as on disk), `provenance` (`person` or `agent`).

Journal `approval` line (`journal.EventApproval`), written before the push so
the one checkpoint commit holds the approval file, the line and any draft the
store had uncommitted: `ts` (nanosecond), `event`, `provenance`, `ref` (the
first 12 hex of `brief_sha256`), `summary` (`Name <email>`). No `slice`, no
`session`. No ledger line. The line is written only when the approval file is.

Commit message of that push: `<ticket>: brief approved`.

## Decisions

- **Duplicate and covered-work measure.** `recall.Similarity`: lowercase runs of
  letters and digits, two or more characters, minus a short English stopword
  list (`similar.go`), no stemming, Jaccard of the two sets, `recall.Threshold`
  = 0.5, scores shown to two decimals. A query is scored against the title and
  against the whole body and the higher wins (`in` says which). A long body
  scores low against a short query, so in practice the body matters when it
  is short, as a chart entry body is.
- **Open means not published** (no `publish-done` line; the `done` state of
  `internal/ready`). `recallTickets` reuses `readTicketData` and `ready.Tickets`
  of `jig next`, so a broken ticket.yaml fails recall as it fails `jig next`.
- **Covered work compares goals with titles only**: shipped tickets (ticket.yaml
  title) and ledger.md entry headings (`## <ticket> - <title>`). A ticket
  published before records carried a title is known by its ledger heading
  alone. Journals are read only for tickets whose title is near a goal.
- **Fog** is the first heading at any level that starts "Not yet specified" or
  contains "fog", up to the next heading of the same or higher level; items
  are bullets, numbered lines or paragraphs; headings in code fences are
  ignored. At most 30 items per chart (`more` counts the rest), 300 characters
  each. The intake template heading `Not yet specified (fog)` is the contract.
- **Ledger answers** are the `**Answers:**` bullets of each entry, the text kept
  whole as `<question> -> <answer>` (the arrow can occur in both, so it is not
  split). With no query: the latest 10 entries that have answers, newest
  first. With a query: entries whose title and answers share a word with it,
  most words first, newest first among equals, at most 10.
- **Platform documents** are the `.md` files below the project config
  `platform` directory (default `platform/`; a path leaving the store is
  refused), dot directories skipped, at most 40, first 256 KiB read.
- **No `Sync`** in `recall` or `validate`. `validate` pushes only when it
  records, and the push pulls and rebases. A stale local brief is approved by
  its hash, so the worst a stale read does is leave the approval unmatched.
- **Idempotent approval**, described above, so repeated validate runs in an
  intake loop do not make a commit each.

## ADR impact

ADR 0032 (or the doc that says how a brief is approved): `jig validate` is the
approval. A valid brief is recorded in `approval.yaml` with the identity git
resolves, the hash of brief.md and whether a person or an agent ran it; any
edit to brief.md withdraws it; `jig claim` checks the hash (D8). There is no
`jig approve`. `jig recall` is read-only: the planner sees duplicates, fog,
platform documents and past answers before it writes a ticket.
